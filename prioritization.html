Plan Guided by a Vision

When we want to move fast, how do we stay in tune with what we are about?

My own values guide me as we go about this endeavor.

I remind myself that through connection we can create a space where we can be open about our differences in how we see things.

Making space for our differences, and bringing us to an agreement when the time is right towards peace. Finding balance, allowing plenty of time for diverse perspectives, and when that time is right deciding how we move forward.

Remembering to check in and be honest with how much I can give here, while appreciating what I am gaining from this experience.

Our mission: why are we here?

Our plan will show us how to go about our vision, but before we dive into aligning on a vision and a plan, we first need to answer the question, what is our mission? What are we doing today?

For our example I will conjure up a tool that improves on a regulatory workflow in HR. This company's mission is We take the complexity out of HR compliance.

Our team specifically has a product working on automating workflows for our users, our mission that we derive out of the company mission is:
To simplify the lives of HR professionals navigating regulatory processes through workflow automation.

That's where we are today. Now where do we want to go?

Our vision: where do we want to go?

Our vision is the place we dream, this is where hope comes in, it is where we want to go and do better. For our example team they want to support the business in staying ahead of the market when it comes to regulatory HR workflow automation, they want to continue learning where they can contribute to a company vision that envisions a world where HR professionals can focus on what matters, without carrying the weight of what doesn't.

But our team also are carrying product debt that accumulated when the business sprinted forward to stay ahead of the market, and the codebase they are owning became hard to extend, changes in regulations take up crucial time adapting the codebase, so our team dreams to take care of their day to day peace of mind in simplifying the way regulations are updated.

This brings our team to a vision:

A world where regulatory change is no longer a source of toil or uncertainty, for HR professionals or for us.

This is a good start for us, we know what our mission is as a team, and we dreamt together where we want to go.

Here comes the flood, everyone on the team would have many great ideas of how we can set out to accomplish our mission, vision, dreams and hopes for a better future. The next question is, what to go about first, how do we prioritize?

Prioritization based on values

As a business we often set values to differentiate our company, to show what we are about, what matters to us.

We also set goals, what is the next thing we want to achieve in order to continue going about our mission and pursue our vision. For those goals we set KPIs, objective tools to measure our impact in working towards those goals.

We are going to use both when we come up with our plan, our values and our KPIs, to measure the impact of individual ideas we have. Comparing as well the relative effort to understand which idea will give us the highest return on our investment.

For our example team, they decided that these are the values that they can agree on that guide them in staying aligned with their vision and how they work together on delivering value:

  • We value doing it right rather than fast.
    This came out of their experience of accumulating product debt and being extremely slowed down by it and this impacting them on a daily basis.
  • We make the complex feel simple
    This value is in alignment with what they see their mission to be, what their product specializes in.
  • We respect the human behind the process.
    It is important for our team to remind ourselves of the humans impacted by our product, empathize with the HR professionals, the customers, as well as the employees impacted by the product, the end users.
  • We gain trust through transparency.
    Our vision is all about building the trust of our users in our product, giving the users the sense that we take care of the regulatory workflows that we are capable of being automated and you don't need to worry about it. Trust us that we will ask you where your input is needed. The team understands they need to work for this trust, and that having this as a value will lead them to making choices that would materialize their vision.

We can now use these values, when measuring the impact of various ideas we want to address on our roadmap.

Utilizing as well a success metric, a Key Performance Indicator, would let us know if we are going in the right direction towards our vision.

The best indicator, or KPI, that tells us that we are working towards our world where when change happens it isn't a pain to us or our users, is calculating the percentage of workflows that completed successfully, correctly and on time. Then we know that we are earning the trust of our customers.
