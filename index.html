My Values

Connection

I believe in Connection leading to shared vision, building trust, creating space for vulnerability and a sense of belonging and community. Listening and respecting people’s differences, in their creative process, in the way they think and in addressing their personal needs and social needs.

Peace

I have learned to make space for divergence as a natural part of life, and to find the right time to guide towards convergence to encourage the merge of those diverse perspectives

Reciprocity

I strive to give what I can, and take just as much as I need. An attitude of reciprocity, respect and appreciation towards the gifts of others, and nature, out of which the urge to give stems.

Balance

I allow myself to fail and learn from my failures, in working towards finding balance, the golden path, between intervening and letting things play out, or between allowing space for difficult feelings, while also creating a sense of hope and optimism to work towards where we want to go.
